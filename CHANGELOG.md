## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-23

The first release.

- A courier office of the thirties drawn in code: the depot in its street, the sky by the hour, a lamp that lights up at night, a clock with the real time.
- The waybill: lines with whose order they are (a stamp for everyone at home), amounts, weight and the cold box from a goods catalogue, rush orders, notes in the margin, suggestions from what was ordered before.
- "Share → Errand" from any messenger: a list becomes orders line by line; "!" or "urgent" makes a rush. The waybill can be sent back as text.
- Accepting an order: money for the run and the shops on the way; a warning when the waybill is heavier than you carry.
- The run: the waybill in each shop's own shelf order, learned from how you tick things off, the cold box last; a string bag that fills up; empty shelves; prices noted on the way; things picked up without an order (the first is a treat).
- Delivery: the receipt, the weather, a stamp and a payslip (lines, kilos, rushes, nothing forgotten, budget, weather, big runs). Forgotten lines ride over marked "not delivered".
- The depot: fourteen things to buy with tokens, four of them vehicles; ranks open the bigger ones.
- The personnel file: seven ranks, the record and fourteen badges.
- The merchandiser's notebook (what went up, where it's cheaper) and the archive with a report for each month.
- A guide on the first run and behind the "?". Six currencies. English and Russian. No permissions at all.
